# Human-Likeness Rubric

Score each category from 0 to 5.

- 0: unusable or absent
- 1: severe artifacts or implausible behavior
- 2: frequent visible failures
- 3: acceptable in short clips with noticeable issues
- 4: strong result with minor issues
- 5: consistently natural and convincing under benchmark conditions

Categories:

- Lip sync
- Face realism
- Eye/gaze life
- Expression naturalness
- Head motion
- Upper-body/hand gesture
- Listening behavior
- Turn-taking suitability
- Long-duration consistency
- Overall conversational presence

Review notes should separate technical artifacts from permission, consent, and publication constraints.
